# Green Village Anuradhapura — Website Research

Research date: 14 August 2026

## Executive recommendation

Build **Green Village Anuradhapura** as a combined homestay, local-guide, and village-experience website—not as a generic tourism portfolio.

The most valuable promise is:

> **Stay with a local family. Explore Anuradhapura with a local teacher. Experience the real village life of Sri Lanka.**

The website should make three actions immediately clear:

1. **Stay at Green Village** — link to the Airbnb/Booking.com accommodation listing.
2. **Tour Anuradhapura with Gunarathna** — link to the Airbnb Experience or send an enquiry.
3. **Explore village experiences** — cooking, village life, Wilpattu safari coordination, cycling, and community connection.

## What the supplied Airbnb pages show

### 1. Green Village homestay

The [Airbnb accommodation listing](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/13886001) currently presents:

- A private guesthouse room in Thalawa for up to four guests, with one bedroom, two beds, a private bathroom, air conditioning, and free parking.
- A “Guest favorite” rating of **4.96/5 from 67 reviews** at the time of research.
- A peaceful home and garden surrounded by coconut palms, with easy access to Anuradhapura.
- Participation in family life, including cooking Sri Lankan dishes and eating together.
- Optional Anuradhapura guiding, Wilpattu safari coordination, cooking classes, and opportunities to meet students.
- A social-impact story: income helps Gunarathna provide English teaching and educational supplies to local children.

The review themes visible on Airbnb are especially valuable: hospitality, feeling like family, local culture, food, historical knowledge, communication, and good value. This is stronger and more distinctive than simply offering a room.

### 2. Local Anuradhapura tour

The [Airbnb Experience](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/experiences/471573) currently presents:

- “Tour Anuradhapura with a local tour guide.”
- **4.97/5 from 100 reviews** at the time of research.
- A roughly 5.5-hour English-language tour, with a maximum group size of ten.
- Stops and themes including the Sacred Bo Tree, stupas, Isurumuniya temple, local shops, crafts, spices, Buddhism, and Anuradhapura’s history.
- Gunarathna’s differentiators: 16 years of hotel experience, fluent English, local knowledge, teaching work, friendliness, and personal storytelling.

Guests repeatedly value knowledge, kindness, clear English, calm pacing, organisation, and the ability to make monuments and Buddhist history meaningful.

### Combined conclusion

These are not two separate businesses. They form one strong visitor journey:

**Discover the family → trust the host → choose a stay or tour → add a village experience → support the local community.**

### Is there already a website like this?

Yes. Personal guide sites, homestay sites, and community-tourism sites already exist. However, few combine all four of Green Village’s strongest assets in one focused offer:

- A real family homestay.
- A highly reviewed local heritage guide.
- Hands-on village and cooking experiences.
- A believable educational/community-impact story.

That combination is the competitive advantage.

## Brand-name finding

“Green Village” alone is too broad and is already used by several Sri Lankan hospitality businesses. Search results include [Green Village Homestay in Dambulla](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lk/green-village-homestay.en-gb.html) and [Green Village Safari Resort](https://greenvillagesafariresort.com/). Your property also appears as [Green Village Anuradhpura in Talawa](https://www.booking.com/hotel/lk/home-stay-in-anuradhapura-talawa1.html).

Recommended public name:

**Green Village Anuradhapura**

Recommended signature line:

**Stay & Explore with Gunarathna**

Possible tagline:

**Live like a local. Discover the ancient city. Leave as family.**

Before buying a domain or registering a trademark, check its current availability. Good domain directions include `greenvillageanuradhapura...` or `staywithgunarathna...`; availability is not confirmed by this report.

## Recommended audience

Primary visitors:

- International solo travellers and couples seeking authentic local experiences.
- Cultural travellers visiting Anuradhapura’s sacred city.
- Families who value a safe and welcoming host family.
- Slow-travel visitors who want food, village life, nature, and human connection.
- Travellers deciding between a guide, homestay, cooking activity, or safari add-on.

Their main questions are: Is this trustworthy? What exactly can I do? Is the room comfortable? Does the guide speak good English? How far is it from Anuradhapura? What is included? How do I book?

## Recommended sitemap

### Home

- Emotional hero image and one-sentence promise.
- Two main buttons: **Book a Stay** and **Explore Tours**.
- Short introduction to Gunarathna and his family.
- Review proof from Airbnb and other platforms, with links and the date the ratings were checked.
- Featured cards: homestay, sacred-city tour, village cooking, and safari.
- Short community-impact section.
- Map/location and final booking call to action.

### Stay

- Room, beds, bathroom, air conditioning, meals, parking, check-in, and guest capacity.
- Photo gallery showing the full guest journey.
- “What it feels like” section: quiet garden, family welcome, cooking, and village sounds.
- Travel time and transport guidance for Anuradhapura and Talawa.
- Clear **Book on Airbnb** and **View on Booking.com** buttons.

### Tours & Experiences

- Anuradhapura Sacred City Tour.
- Village cooking and family meal.
- Village walk/cycle and local-life experience.
- Wilpattu safari coordination.
- Each card must show duration, language, group size, meeting point, what is included/not included, clothing guidance, accessibility, and booking method.

### About Gunarathna

- Friendly portrait and personal story.
- Hotel experience, guiding experience, English ability, and local expertise.
- Family introduction.
- Teaching and community work explained factually and respectfully.

### Reviews

- Short, permission-cleared excerpts or manually entered testimonials.
- Link to the original Airbnb listings for verification.
- Organise by themes: hospitality, historical knowledge, family experience, food, and communication.

### Anuradhapura Guide

- Sacred sites and practical visitor advice.
- Respectful clothing guidance.
- Getting around, suggested visit length, and official ticket links.
- Original local recommendations rather than copied destination text.

### Contact / Plan Your Visit

- WhatsApp, email, phone, and a short enquiry form.
- Ask only for name, contact method, dates, number of guests, and interests.
- Show response expectations and privacy notice.

## Visual and image research

### What works in the current Airbnb images

The accommodation listing’s strongest image is the wide exterior photograph: it immediately communicates a real village home, veranda, shade, and greenery. Supporting images show the porch, two-bed room, garden/river scenery, and interior. The tour listing is stronger emotionally because it shows Gunarathna together with guests and recognisable Anuradhapura monuments.

### Current gaps

- The accommodation images communicate the building better than the experience.
- Lighting, framing, colour, and image quality vary.
- There are too few strong photographs of hospitality, cooking, guiding, and family interaction.
- The tour selfie builds trust, but a professionally composed host-and-guests image would make a stronger website hero.
- Guest faces and children require clear permission before being used on a public website.

### Essential new shot list

1. Gunarathna guiding two or three guests near a recognisable Anuradhapura monument.
2. A warm, natural portrait of Gunarathna.
3. House exterior and garden during soft morning or late-afternoon light.
4. Clean wide shots of the bedroom and private bathroom.
5. Family welcoming guests on the veranda.
6. Cooking curry, roti, coconut sambol, or string hoppers together.
7. A shared table with a colourful Sri Lankan meal.
8. Village walk, bicycle, river, coconut palms, birds, and everyday rural scenes.
9. Safari/transport experience, only where permissions allow.
10. Community teaching or educational support, photographed respectfully with written consent from adults and guardians.

Capture landscape, portrait, and square versions. Keep high-resolution original files. The hero image should have calm space for text and should still crop well on a phone.

### Image rights and accessibility

Use original photos owned by Green Village or photos for which written reuse permission is available. Do not copy the Airbnb interface, Airbnb branding, other guests’ profile images, or photos whose ownership is uncertain. Airbnb’s [Host Terms](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2857) restrict copying content accessed through the platform unless the user is the legal owner or has permission.

For website delivery:

- Export responsive AVIF/WebP versions while preserving the originals.
- Use real `<img>`/`<picture>` elements, descriptive filenames, and useful alt text; these practices also help image discovery in search. See [Google Image SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images).
- Give informative images short descriptions and decorative images empty alt text, following [W3C image accessibility guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/).
- Never put important information only inside an image.

## Design direction

### Mood

Warm, natural, personal, trustworthy, and quietly premium—not luxury and not a busy travel marketplace.

### Suggested palette

- Deep forest green — trust and landscape.
- Rice-field/sage green — secondary surfaces.
- Warm clay or terracotta — buttons and cultural warmth.
- Coconut/cream — background.
- Charcoal — readable text.

Use colour contrast that passes accessibility checks. Avoid pale green text on cream.

### Typography

Use an expressive but readable serif for important headings and a clean sans-serif for body text. The design should favour large photography, generous spacing, short paragraphs, and clear cards.

### Best home-page hero concept

**Headline:** Stay local. Explore ancient Anuradhapura.

**Supporting line:** A peaceful village homestay and personal tours with Gunarathna, a local teacher, storyteller, and Superhost.

**Primary button:** Explore the Homestay

**Secondary button:** Tour with Gunarathna

## Content and conversion priorities

1. Put Gunarathna’s face, name, and credibility near the top.
2. Show the two main choices—stay and tour—without making visitors search.
3. Use real review evidence, but date dynamic ratings and link to the source.
4. Explain distance and transport clearly; uncertainty about Talawa versus Anuradhapura may otherwise reduce bookings.
5. State inclusions, exclusions, entry fees, meeting points, duration, and clothing requirements clearly.
6. Place a booking or enquiry action after every major section.
7. Make WhatsApp useful but do not let it replace accessible, structured information.
8. Do not encourage Airbnb guests to move an existing reservation or payment off-platform to avoid fees. If direct booking is added later, publish independent prices, terms, cancellation rules, privacy information, and a secure payment process.

## SEO direction

Target specific intent, not the very broad phrase “tourism guide portfolio.” Useful page themes include:

- Local tour guide in Anuradhapura.
- Anuradhapura private tour.
- Anuradhapura homestay with local family.
- Village experience near Anuradhapura.
- Sri Lankan cooking experience in Anuradhapura.
- Green Village Anuradhapura.
- Gunarathna Anuradhapura guide.

Technical essentials: fast mobile loading, responsive images, descriptive page titles, one clear H1 per page, crawlable HTML content, LocalBusiness/LodgingBusiness and TouristTrip structured data where appropriate, an XML sitemap, Open Graph images, HTTPS, and analytics with consent controls where required.

## Recommended first version

The first release should be a polished responsive website with:

- Home, Stay, Tours, About, Reviews, Anuradhapura Guide, and Contact pages.
- Original image gallery.
- Airbnb and Booking.com links for accommodation.
- Airbnb Experience link for the tour.
- WhatsApp/email enquiry for custom plans.
- A lightweight content system only if Gunarathna needs to update tours, photos, or guide articles regularly.

Do not begin with a complex custom booking engine. Establish the brand, trust, photography, and enquiry flow first; add direct availability and payment only after the operational rules are ready.

## Final positioning statement

**Green Village Anuradhapura is a peaceful family homestay and gateway to authentic local experiences, where guests explore the ancient city with Gunarathna, share village food and daily life, and contribute to a positive local story.**
